Restructure the Night phase into three sequential private action windows in this order: Mafia first, then Doctor, then Detective.
Step 1 — Mafia's turn: Show the Mafia a private targeting screen with a 30-second timer and a list of all living players excluding themselves. They select one to eliminate. All other players see a generic "The town is asleep… stay quiet" waiting screen with no timer visible. If the Mafia does not select before the timer expires, no attack happens this night.
Step 2 — Doctor's turn: Immediately after the Mafia's timer ends, show the Doctor a private targeting screen with a fresh 30-second timer and a list of all living players including themselves. They select one to protect. All other players remain on the generic waiting screen. The Doctor receives no private confirmation of whether their save worked — they only learn the outcome through the public night summary shown to everyone at the end of Night.
Step 3 — Detective's turn: Immediately after the Doctor's timer ends, show the Detective a private targeting screen with a fresh 30-second timer and a list of all living players excluding themselves. They select one to investigate. On selection, immediately show the Detective a private inline message — "[Name] is Mafia" or "[Name] is not Mafia" — visible only to them within the same screen, before the timer expires. This result is never shared publicly. All other players remain on the generic waiting screen.
Night resolution after all three timers complete: if the Doctor's protection choice matches the Mafia's target, the target survives; otherwise the target is eliminated — including if the Doctor's timer expired without a selection.
At the transition from Night to Day, before the discussion timer starts, display a full-screen night summary screen to all players. Reveal the following lines sequentially using a typewriter animation — each character types out one at a time, with a natural pause between each line completing and the next one starting, to build suspense:
Line 1 (always shown): If the Mafia made no attack this night — "The town slept peacefully. No one was harmed." and skip Lines 2 and 3. If someone was attacked and killed — "The town woke to find [Name] dead in the streets." If someone was attacked but survived — "The town stirred… someone was attacked in the night."
Line 2 (always shown when an attack occurred): If the Doctor successfully protected the target — "But somehow, they survived the night." If the Doctor attempted to protect but chose the wrong person — "The Doctor tried to save someone… but failed." If the Doctor's timer expired without a selection — "The Doctor tried to save someone… but failed."
Line 3 (always shown last when an attack occurred, flavour only): "The Detective watches silently, taking notes." — never mention who was investigated or what was found.
After all lines have fully typed out, show a "Begin Day" button that the host must press to proceed, giving players time to absorb the summary before discussion starts. Keep all existing noir styling — dark background, amber/red accent color for the typewriter text animation.

Delete your Mafia Wars account

Developer: Mafia Wars · Contact: sayedarif.dev@proton.me

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open Mafia Wars (the Android app or the website) and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap Delete account at the bottom, next to the Privacy Policy and Terms links.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Can't sign in?

Email sayedarif.dev@proton.me from the address you signed up with, using the subject “Mafia Wars account deletion”. We delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address, display name, date of birth and password hash.
  • All login sessions, your profile and game statistics.
  • Chat messages you sent.

What is kept

Finished match results stay as anonymous records (shown as “Deleted player”, with no link to you) so other players' game history stays consistent. We may keep a minimal record where the law requires it or to prevent abuse. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

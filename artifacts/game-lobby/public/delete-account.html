Delete your Mafia Town account

App: Mafia: Town Has a Problem · Developer: Sayyed Muhammad Arif Hussain · Contact: sayedarif.dev@proton.me

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open Mafia Town (the Android app or the website) and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap Delete account at the bottom, next to the Privacy Policy and Terms links.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Can't sign in?

Email sayedarif.dev@proton.me from the address you signed up with, using the subject “Mafia Town account deletion”. We delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address, display name, date of birth, and password hash.
  • All login sessions, your profile, and your game statistics.
  • Chat messages you sent.
  • Any coins and cosmetic items on the account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Finished match results that other players also appear in stay as anonymous records: your seat shows “Deleted player”, with no link to you, so their history stays correct.
  • Reports and suspension records involving the account are kept for up to 12 months, only to act on repeat abuse and handle appeals.
  • A minimal record where the law requires it.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

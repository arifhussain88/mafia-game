Privacy Policy

Mafia: Town Has a Problem (“Mafia Town”)
Effective date: 10 November 2026
Language: English

This policy explains what information Mafia: Town Has a Problem (“the game”) collects, why, who else processes it, and how you can access or delete it. The game is a free multiplayer social-deduction game, offered as an Android app and as a website. Both are a window onto the same game server. The game is offered to players worldwide and is not directed at children under 13.

The rights in this policy are available to every player. Notes for particular regions are included only where local law asks us to be more specific, and they do not reduce the rights offered to everyone else.

Who we are

The game is published by Sayyed Muhammad Arif Hussain, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). For privacy requests, email sayedarif.dev@proton.me. The developer lives in Pakistan, which is where the game is operated from; that is not a limit on who may play.

Information we collect

We collect only what the game needs to run your account, check your age, keep matches fair and safe, and show you your own stats.

  • Account details. Email address, display name, and password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we never keep a readable copy.
  • Date of birth. Used to apply the minimum age. It is stored with your account so the age check can be kept, and it is never shown to other players.
  • Game activity. Room codes, the display names of people in a room, roles, night actions, votes, and whether a player is connected. Other people in your room see your display name, your votes once they are revealed, and your role when the rules reveal it.
  • Chat. Messages you send in a room are shown to the players allowed to see them (for example, the whole town during the day, only the Mafia in Mafia night chat, or only eliminated players in ghost chat). We keep a copy of chat to investigate reports and abuse.
  • Match history and stats. For each finished match: the room, your role, whether your side won, whether you survived, and a short outcome (for example “saved a player” or “voted out on day 2”). We use this to show your profile stats. Only you see your own profile.
  • Reports and safety records. When someone reports a player or a message, we store who reported, who was reported, the message or reason, the room, and the time. If we suspend an account, we keep a record of the suspension.
  • Session data. A login token so you stay signed in.
  • Technical data. The server may log an IP address, device or browser type, and basic request or error data, so the service can run and so we can investigate abuse or outages.
  • Advertising data, only when ads are shown. See Advertising below.

We do not ask for your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or payment card details. We do not sell personal information.

How we use it

  • Create your account, sign you in, and provide the game you asked for.
  • Apply the minimum age.
  • Place you in a room, run the match, and let you rejoin your seat after a dropped connection.
  • Show you your profile, stats, and recent matches.
  • Keep the game safe: filter offensive words in names and chat, review reports, and suspend accounts that break the Terms.
  • Answer support, access, correction, and deletion requests.
  • Protect the service against abuse, such as repeated failed sign-ins or attempts to disrupt a room.
  • When ads are shown, show them and limit how often you see them.

Sharing and invites

The game can open your device’s share menu so you can invite friends. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. The invite contains only the room code and a link to the game; we do not read your contacts or learn who you shared it with.

AI players

In Quick Play, empty seats may be filled by computer-controlled players so a match can start. They are marked in the game. They are not people, and no personal data is collected about or by them.

Advertising

The game is free and may show ads. When ads are shown:

  • They are served by Google AdMob. AdMob processes your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and app-usage information to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google’s consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game’s settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Ads are not targeted at children. Ads that give an in-game reward are always optional.

Until ads are switched on, no advertising ID is collected.

Virtual items and purchases

If the game offers coins or cosmetic items, your balance and items are stored on our server with your account. If real-money purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. We receive confirmation of the purchase, never your card details.

Who else processes it

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure runs the game server. Live rooms, votes, and timers stay in that server’s memory while a match runs.
  • Neon (managed Postgres, aws-ap-southeast-1, Singapore) stores accounts, sessions, profiles, match history, retained chat, and reports. Privacy information: neon.tech/privacy-policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game’s fonts, so your device sends its IP address and browser details to Google when it fetches them. Privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play distributes the Android app and, if offered, processes purchases.
  • Google AdMob, only when ads are shown (see above).

We do not use a separate analytics product. These providers process data under their own terms. We have not signed separate Standard Contractual Clauses with players; where a provider offers its own international-transfer terms, those terms apply to that provider’s processing.

Where it is processed

Data may be processed in Pakistan, where the developer operates the service; on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, where the server runs; in Singapore, where Neon stores durable data; and by Google in the United States and other countries for fonts, the Play Store, and ads. Those places may not provide the same level of legal protection as the country where you live.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, those locations may not have an adequacy decision covering every transfer. By creating an account, you consent to this transfer and storage so that we can provide the game. You may withdraw that consent by deleting your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, and stats: until you delete the account or we close it.
  • Live room and match data: only while that room exists on the server.
  • Match history and retained chat: until you delete your account. In matches other players also played, your seat is then anonymised (see below).
  • Reports and suspension records: up to 12 months from the report or suspension, even if an account involved is deleted, so that we can act on repeat abuse and handle appeals. Only what is needed for that purpose is kept.
  • Login tokens: until you log out, the token expires (30 days), or the account is deleted.
  • Security and error logs containing an IP address: no longer than 90 days, unless needed longer to investigate abuse or to comply with law.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may email sayedarif.dev@proton.me from the address on your account to:

  • Get a copy of the account data we hold about you.
  • Correct your display name or email.
  • Delete your account and the personal information tied to it.
  • Withdraw consent to processing based on consent, including the international transfer above and personalised ads.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have a compelling reason to continue, such as investigating abuse.
  • Appeal a suspension.

Deleting your account. Sign in, tap Delete account at the bottom of the home screen, and confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate. Instructions are also at /delete-account.html. If you can’t sign in, email us with the subject “Mafia Town account deletion” and we will delete it within 30 days. We delete your email, display name, date of birth, password hash, login tokens, profile and stats, and the chat messages you sent. In finished-match records that other players also appear in, your seat is kept but anonymised: the link to your account is removed and your name is replaced with “Deleted player”, so other players’ results stay correct. Report and suspension records are kept as described above. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

We will not charge you for a reasonable request, and we will not treat you differently for making one.

Players in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Where the data-protection law of your country applies, the developer named above is the controller. We rely on:

  • Contract: to create your account, run the game, and show your stats.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, moderate chat and names, act on reports, prevent repeat abuse, limit ad frequency, and understand outages. These interests are limited to running a small multiplayer game and do not override your rights.
  • Consent: for the international transfers described above and, where required, for personalised ads.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information to comply with law.

You may complain to the data-protection authority in your country, in addition to contacting us. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner’s Office; in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner; in the EEA, see the list published by the European Data Protection Board.

Players in the United States

We do not sell personal information. When ads are shown, Google AdMob uses your advertising ID to deliver and measure ads; you can opt out of personalised ads with the consent options in the game or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings. The access, correction, and deletion rights above are offered to every player, including in California and other US states. Offering them is a choice; it is not a statement that a particular state law applies to this small, independently run game.

Age

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, or older if the law where you live requires it. The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe we have, email sayedarif.dev@proton.me and we will delete it.

Security

Traffic between your device and the game is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and access to account data is restricted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. The English text controls. If a change materially expands what we collect or who we share it with, we will tell you in the game before it applies to you.
